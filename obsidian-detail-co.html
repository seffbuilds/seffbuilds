The Method

What happens between drop-off and delivery.

Stage 01

Inspection

Paint depth is measured with a gauge and defects mapped under 6500K lighting before any work begins.

Stage 02

Decontamination

Foam pre-wash, iron remover, and clay treatment strip away everything bonded to the surface.

Stage 03

Correction

Machine polishing in measured stages, checked panel by panel until the finish reads clear.

Stage 04

Protection

Coating or sealant applied in a dust-controlled bay, then cured under infrared before delivery.
